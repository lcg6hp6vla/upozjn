What are the primary causes of excessive burping?

Explanation

Excessive burping can happen for a few reasons. One common cause is swallowing too much air, which is known as aerophagia. This can occur when eating or drinking too quickly, chewing gum, or smoking. Another cause can be certain digestive problems.

Causes of Excessive Burping

Aerophagia: This is when you swallow a lot of air, usually from eating or drinking too fast.

Digestive Issues: Conditions like gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) can also lead to more frequent burping.

Lifestyle Factors: Habits such as chewing gum or smoking can increase air swallowing.
