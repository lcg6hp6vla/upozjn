Non-stop burping at night is often caused by changes in body position and muscle relaxation during sleep, which affect how air is swallowed and released. Lying flat can trap swallowed air in the stomach, while a relaxed lower esophageal sphincter may allow gas to escape upward more easily. Other common triggers include eating late, acid reflux (GERD), certain foods, and stress.

Explanation

Excessive burping, especially at night, can be influenced by several factors related to sleep and body position. When you lie down, the way your body is positioned can change how air moves through your digestive system. This might lead to more air being swallowed or trapped, resulting in more burping.

Factors Influencing Nighttime Burping:

Body Position: Lying down can change how your body handles swallowed air, possibly leading to more burping.

Relaxation: Sleep can relax muscles in the digestive tract, affecting how air is released.
