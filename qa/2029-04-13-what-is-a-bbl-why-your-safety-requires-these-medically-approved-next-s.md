Is a BBL Safe?

Here is the honest medical answer: A BBL can be performed safely—but it carries higher risks than many other cosmetic surgeries.

Medical studies have shown that Brazilian Butt Lift procedures have historically had a higher complication rate compared to other elective cosmetic surgeries. The most serious risk is fat embolism, a potentially life-threatening condition that occurs when injected fat enters the bloodstream and blocks blood vessels in the lungs.

Because of this risk, medical organizations have developed strict safety guidelines for surgeons, including:

Injecting fat only into the subcutaneous (above muscle) layer

Avoiding deep muscle injections

Using ultrasound guidance during fat placement

Proper patient selection

Board-certified plastic surgery training

These protocols have significantly improved safety outcomes when properly followed.

Medically-Approved Next Steps Before Considering a BBL

If you are seriously considering a Brazilian Butt Lift, your safety depends on following these evidence-based steps.

1. Choose a Board-Certified Plastic Surgeon

This is non-negotiable.

Make sure your surgeon is:

Board-certified in plastic surgery

Operating in an accredited surgical facility

Experienced specifically in BBL procedures

Following updated fat-injection safety guidelines

Ask directly:

How many BBLs have you performed?

What is your complication rate?

Do you use ultrasound guidance?

Where exactly do you inject the fat?

A reputable surgeon will answer clearly and without defensiveness.

2. Get a Full Medical Evaluation

Before surgery, you should have:

A complete medical history review

Blood tests

Evaluation of heart and lung health

Medication review

Discussion of past surgeries

Be completely honest about your health history. Withholding information can increase your risk.

3. Understand the Realistic Outcomes

A BBL:

Cannot guarantee a specific body shape

May require touch-ups

Involves swelling that lasts weeks to months

Results in some fat reabsorption (30–50% is common)

Understanding this reduces disappointment and helps you make a grounded decision.

4. Prepare for Recovery Properly

Recovery from a BBL is significant. You will likely need to:

Avoid sitting directly on your buttocks for several weeks

Sleep on your stomach or side

Wear compression garments

Limit strenuous activity

Take blood clot prevention measures

Skipping these instructions increases your risk of complications.

5. Know the Warning Signs After Surgery

You should seek immediate medical care if you experience:

Sudden shortness of breath

Chest pain

Rapid heartbeat

Severe dizziness

Fainting

Leg swelling or pain

These symptoms can indicate a blood clot or fat embolism and require emergency attention.

Never ignore symptoms that feel severe or unusual. If something feels wrong, speak to a doctor immediately.
