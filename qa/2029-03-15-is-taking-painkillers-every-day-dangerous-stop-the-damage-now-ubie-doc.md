Taking painkillers every day can be risky and may lead to health problems. Different types of painkillers have different risks:

Opioids: These can cause serious side effects like addiction, drowsiness, and even breathing problems if used daily.

Paracetamol (Acetaminophen): Using it every day can harm your liver, especially if you take more than the recommended dose.

NSAIDs (like ibuprofen): Daily use can lead to stomach problems, kidney issues, and increase the risk of heart problems.

It's important to be careful with painkillers and talk to a doctor if you feel you need them every day.
