Why do I experience a burning sensation on my face?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A burning sensation on your face is often caused by sensitive skin, a condition where the skin overreacts to triggers like weather changes, skincare products, fragrances, or stress. This heightened reactivity can lead to stinging, tingling, redness, or a warm, burning feeling—especially on the cheeks, forehead, or around the nose. Other common causes include rosacea, contact dermatitis, sunburn, or nerve-related irritation.

Because facial burning can stem from many different conditions—some minor, others requiring medical attention—it's important to identify the underlying cause before choosing a treatment. Guessing wrong could make symptoms worse.
