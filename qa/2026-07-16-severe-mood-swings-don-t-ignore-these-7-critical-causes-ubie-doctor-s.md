Severe mood swings can be caused by different mental health conditions. Research shows that individuals with panic disorder often experience mood swings. Similarly, anxiety disorders can also lead to mood changes. Managing these mood swings can be challenging, but mood stabilizers might be helpful.

Panic Disorder: People with panic disorder may have sudden changes in mood due to intense feelings of fear or anxiety.

Anxiety Disorders: These disorders can cause emotional ups and downs, impacting a person's mood significantly.

Mood Stabilizers: These medications may target specific pathways in the brain to help control mood swings.

If you or someone you know is experiencing severe mood swings, it is essential to seek help from a mental health professional. To better understand what might be causing your symptoms, you can take a free symptom checker assessment to get personalized insights and guidance on next steps.
