What should I consider if my period is 3 days late?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A period that is 3 days late is typically within the range of normal cycle variation. Common causes include stress, travel, changes in sleep or exercise, hormonal shifts, or an early sign of pregnancy. Most menstrual cycles vary by a few days from month to month, so a short delay is rarely a cause for concern on its own.

However, if your period remains late or you notice symptoms like nausea, breast tenderness, fatigue, cramping, or unusual discharge, it's worth investigating further.
