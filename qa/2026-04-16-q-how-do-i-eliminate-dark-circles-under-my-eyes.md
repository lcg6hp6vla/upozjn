Dark circles can be a tough issue to address. The skin under the eye is about ten times thinner than the rest of the face. Over time, the fat deposits protecting our eye area can dissolve leaving the blood vessels more apparent under the thin skin, attributing to that darkness on our lower lids. Smoking, drinking, stress, and allergies can dilate and darken the capillaries further, but the number one contributing factor to dark circles is usually hereditary.
