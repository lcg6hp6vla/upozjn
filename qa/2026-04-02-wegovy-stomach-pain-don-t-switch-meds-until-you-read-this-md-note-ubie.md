Why Does Wegovy Cause Stomach Pain?

The key driver is slowed gastric emptying. Here's how it leads to discomfort:

Delayed EmptyingGLP-1 agonists significantly slow how quickly your stomach empties. Food sits longer, stretching the stomach wall and triggering pain or a "heavy" feeling.

Altered Gut MotilityReduced coordination of stomach and intestinal muscles can cause spasms or cramping.

Hormonal EffectsElevated GLP-1 influences other gut hormones (like cholecystokinin), which can heighten sensations of fullness and discomfort.

Individual SensitivitySome people have more sensitive gastrointestinal tracts or underlying conditions (e.g., gastroparesis) that make them more prone to pain.

When Is Stomach Pain Normal vs. Concerning?

Understanding the difference can help you decide when to ride out side effects and when to seek medical advice.

Normal, Mild Discomfort

Should You Switch Medications?

Switching away from Wegovy may be appropriate if:

Side effects are severe despite optimal management.

You have a pre-existing GI condition (e.g., gastroparesis, inflammatory bowel disease).

You experience red-flag symptoms (see "Concerning Symptoms" above).

Alternative Options

Other GLP-1 agonists: Some patients tolerate drugs like Saxenda (liraglutide) or off-label Ozempic (semaglutide formulation for diabetes) differently.

Dual agonists: Medications in development target both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, potentially with different side effect profiles.

Non-GLP-1 therapies: Orlistat, bupropion/naltrexone (Contrave), and newer agents may suit those intolerant to GLP-1 drugs.

Always discuss switching or adding therapies with your healthcare provider. They can weigh benefits, risks, and your personal health history.
