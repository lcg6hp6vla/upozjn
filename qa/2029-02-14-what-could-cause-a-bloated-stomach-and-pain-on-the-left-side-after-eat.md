Explanation

Experiencing a bloated stomach and pain on the left side after eating can be uncomfortable and may have several causes. One possible cause is Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), a common condition that affects the digestive system, leading to symptoms like bloating and abdominal pain. IBS can make the gut more sensitive and may cause discomfort after meals.

Another potential cause is related to the thyroid gland, which can influence the gut. Thyroid problems can affect how the digestive system works, leading to symptoms such as bloating and pain.
