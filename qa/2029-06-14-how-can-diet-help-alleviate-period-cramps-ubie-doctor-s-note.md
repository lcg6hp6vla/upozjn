Zinc:

Zinc is a mineral that can help reduce period pain. Eating foods like meat, shellfish, nuts, and seeds can increase zinc intake. Studies suggest that zinc may help to prevent cramps from becoming too painful.

[Doctor's Note](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/7547512989.html)

Vitamin E:

Vitamin E is another nutrient that may help with cramps. Foods such as nuts, seeds, and green leafy vegetables are good sources of vitamin E. Research indicates that vitamin E can help lessen the intensity of period pain.
