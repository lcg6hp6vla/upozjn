Unilateral tonsil swelling—when one tonsil is noticeably larger than the other—can result from several underlying causes, including bacterial or viral infections (such as tonsillitis, strep throat, or mononucleosis), peritonsillar abscess, tonsil stones, or, in less common cases, tumors or lymphoma. Because the causes range from mild to serious, identifying the reason early is key to getting the right treatment.
