Yes, seizures can wake you from sleep. Sleep-related seizures are common in certain types of epilepsy, including nocturnal frontal lobe epilepsy and juvenile myoclonic epilepsy, where seizures typically occur during sleep or shortly after waking. Symptoms may include sudden awakening, muscle jerks, confusion, unusual movements, or biting the tongue.

Explanation

Seizures can indeed wake a person from sleep, and this is a known occurrence in certain types of epilepsy. Some people experience seizures specifically when they are asleep or during the transition from sleep to waking up.

Epilepsy with Grand Mal on Awakening: This type of epilepsy involves seizures that typically occur as a person wakes up.

First Seizure from Sleep: Some individuals may experience their first seizure while they are asleep, which can be a significant event.

Nocturnal Paroxysmal Arousals: These are episodes where a person wakes up with sudden movements or behaviors, which can be related to seizures.
