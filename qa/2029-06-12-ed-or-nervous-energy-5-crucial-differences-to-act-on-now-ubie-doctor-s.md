Erectile dysfunction (ED) and nervous energy differ in five key ways. ED is a persistent inability to achieve or maintain an erection, driven by vascular, neurologic, or hormonal issues and often linked to cardiometabolic risks. It recurs across encounters for 3+ months and responds to lifestyle changes, PDE5 medications, hormone evaluation, and counseling.

Nervous energy, by contrast, is a short-lived fight-or-flight response causing jitteriness from stress, caffeine, or poor sleep. It's event-triggered and improves with relaxation techniques, cognitive behavioral therapy, rest, and reducing stimulants.
