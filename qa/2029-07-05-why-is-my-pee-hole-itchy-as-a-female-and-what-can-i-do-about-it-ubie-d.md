Explanation

Itching around the urethra in women can be caused by several factors. The most common causes include urinary tract infections (UTIs), vaginal yeast infections, and skin irritation from harsh products. In some cases, skin conditions or sexually transmitted infections may cause urethral itching. Paying attention to other symptoms can help identify the specific cause—for instance, changes in vaginal discharge may point to a yeast infection or sexually transmitted infection. To help relieve symptoms, you might try: • Using warm water (without harsh soaps) when you clean the genital area. • Choosing unscented and gentle hygiene products. • Wearing cotton underwear to help keep the area dry and reduce irritation. • Being cautious with toilet paper—choose gentle, unscented options. Since symptoms can have various causes, it's important to consult your healthcare provider for a proper evaluation. More specific treatment options will depend on what's causing the irritation.
