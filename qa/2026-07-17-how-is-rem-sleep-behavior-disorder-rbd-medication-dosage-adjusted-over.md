REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) is typically treated with medications such as melatonin, clonazepam, sodium oxybate, or rivastigmine. Dosages are individualized and adjusted over time based on two key factors: how effectively the medication reduces RBD symptoms (like dream enactment, vocalizations, and violent movements during sleep) and how well the patient tolerates potential side effects. Regular follow-ups with a healthcare provider are essential to fine-tune treatment and ensure both safety and effectiveness.
