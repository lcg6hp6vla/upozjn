Explanation

Sharp pain during sex, also known as dyspareunia, can happen for different reasons. Physical causes might include infections, like urinary tract infections or yeast infections, which can cause irritation and pain. Hormonal changes, especially those that happen around menopause, can also lead to pain due to dryness and thinning of the vaginal walls.

Emotional factors can also play a role. Stress, anxiety, or past traumatic experiences may contribute to discomfort during intercourse. It's important to consider both physical and emotional aspects when understanding the cause of pain.

To address this pain, it's recommended for individuals to speak with their healthcare provider. They can help identify the specific cause and suggest appropriate treatments, which may include medications, therapy, or lifestyle changes.
