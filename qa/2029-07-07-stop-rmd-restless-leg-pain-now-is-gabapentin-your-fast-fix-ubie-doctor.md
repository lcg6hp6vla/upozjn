Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Gabapentin is not specifically established as a treatment for Sleep-Related Rhythmic Movement Disorder (RMD) accompanied by restless legs or sensory discomfort. Current medical references do not directly support this use, meaning any potential benefit would be considered off-label and should only be explored under the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional.
