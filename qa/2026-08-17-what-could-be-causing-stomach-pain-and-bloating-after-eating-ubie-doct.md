Stomach pain and bloating after eating are common symptoms that can be caused by different factors. According to medical literature, these symptoms might be related to how the digestive system works or how the brain and gut communicate.

Possible Causes:

Digestive System Issues: Sometimes, the stomach and intestines may not move food properly, leading to bloating and pain.
