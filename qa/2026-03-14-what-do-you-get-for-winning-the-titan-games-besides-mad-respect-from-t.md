# What Do You Get for Winning 'The Titan Games,' Besides Mad Respect From The Rock?

Oh, how the time flies! It feels like just yesterday that we were announcing the 64 competitors on The Titan Games, and now, Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson's epic fitness show is gearing up to crown its two winners.

"Proud of all our amazing competitors who left everything they had in the Titan Arena," The Rock wrote in a pair of Instagram posts amping up fans for Thursday night's big finale.

Over the course of ten episodes, The Titan Games—which airs Thursdays on NBC—has whittled down the competition to an elite final eight: Bridger Buckley; Christiana Rugloski; Charity Witt; Tyler Lucas; Jess Griffith; Jackie Wood; James Jean-Louis; and Derik Scott. While the contestants each deserve HUGE RESPECT for being unbelievably fit, in the end, only two competitors—one male and one female—will be crowned the winners and take home a grand prize.
