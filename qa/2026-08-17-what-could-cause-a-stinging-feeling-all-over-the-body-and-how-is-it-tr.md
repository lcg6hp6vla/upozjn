Sometimes, you might feel a stinging or burning sensation over your skin because your nerves are sending extra signals. This is often called neuropathic pain or neurocutaneous dysesthesia. In these cases, the nerves that carry messages from your skin to your brain might be working in a strange way, making you feel pain even when there is no clear injury. Doctors may treat this nerve pain by prescribing medicines such as gabapentin, pregabalin, or certain antidepressants that help reduce extra nerve signals. Sometimes, topical creams that feel cool or soothing might also help. Other treatments can include physical therapy or gentle massage to help calm the nerves and ease discomfort. It is important to talk with your healthcare provider about these symptoms so they can figure out the reason behind the stinging feeling and suggest the best treatment plan for you. Each treatment plan is made to help relieve the pain so you can feel better day by day.
