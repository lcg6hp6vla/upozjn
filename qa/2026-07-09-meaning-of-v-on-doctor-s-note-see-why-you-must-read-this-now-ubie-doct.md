Explanation

Visual impairment is when someone has trouble seeing clearly, and this difficulty isn't completely fixed by wearing glasses, contacts, or even with surgery. It covers a broad range of problems—from slightly blurry vision to significant loss of sight.

When a person has visual impairment, everyday activities such as reading, driving, or recognizing faces might become hard.
