What are concierge healthcare services?

Concierge healthcare services provide patients with direct access to a primary care physician. These services can be found in many different settings, including clinics, hospitals, and private practices. Some of the most common services offered by concierge healthcare providers include physical exams, urgent care, IV treatments, Covid testing, cosmetic treatments, lab draws and lab tests, and telemedicine.

Physical exams (work, annual, routine men’s and women’s exams)

No need to take time off work or scramble to find childcare. They can come to you for physical exams, including annual, routine men’s and women’s exams. It’s important to get a regular physical exam because it helps identify problems before they happen, and it is one way that physicians keep up with your health. That way, they’ll know if something is going on with you that may require more specialized care, such as cardiology, urology, ENT (ear, nose, throat), neurology, etc.

Urgent care

Your health is always a priority, but sometimes you can’t avoid getting sick or injured. That’s where urgent care comes in. Urgent care provides medical care for non-life-threatening injuries and illnesses that need immediate attention. It’s the thing for when you’re feeling under the weather and don’t want to wait in line at the emergency room. Talk to a doctor about your symptoms. They will assess your condition, diagnose any necessary treatments, then get you on the road to recovery as quickly as possible with minimal time wasted.
