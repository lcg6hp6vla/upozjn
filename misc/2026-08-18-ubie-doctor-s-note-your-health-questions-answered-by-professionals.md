How can COPD lead to difficulty walking?

COPD can make walking difficult due to shortness of breath, muscle weakness, and fatigue. These symptoms often alter a person's gait (walking pattern), reduce walking speed, and shorten stride length, making daily activities like walking across a room, climbing stairs, or running errands more challenging. Key ways COPD affects walking: - **Shortness of breath (dyspnea):** Limited lung function reduces oxygen intake, causing breathlessness with movement. - **Muscle weakness:** Reduced activity and low oxygen levels weaken leg muscles over time. - **Fatigue:** Extra effort to breathe drains energy, leading to quicker exhaustion. - **Gait changes:** Slower pace, shorter steps, and poor balance may develop. If you're noticing walking difficulties, shortness of breath, or unusual fatigue, it's important to understand what may be behind your symptoms. How can I get immediate relief from sciatica pain?
