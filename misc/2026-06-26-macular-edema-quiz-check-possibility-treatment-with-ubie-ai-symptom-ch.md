What is Macular Edema?

Macular edema is the accumulation of fluid in the macula, the part of your retina responsible for central vision. It occurs when fluid leads to swelling in the macula, causing blurred vision and vision loss in severe cases. Common causes of macular edema include blockages in retinal arteries or veins and diabetes.

Treatment of Macular Edema

The primary treatment for macular edema involves steroids and anti-inflammatory medications to reduce swelling. These medications are typically administered through eye drops or injections into the eye. Depending on the cause of your macular edema, your doctor may recommend additional procedures, such as laser therapy.
