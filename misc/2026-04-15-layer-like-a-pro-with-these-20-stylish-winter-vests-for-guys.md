What to Look for in Winter Vests

Fabric: The best winter vests should act like a little layer between you and the elements, when it isn’t quite cold enough for a full jacket, but you still need an extra layer. So look for weather-resistant fabrics like nylon, waxed cotton, high pile fleece, or heavy canvas.

Color: There are two ways to go with your vest color. You can stick to dark neutral colors that will match everything, like navy, black, charcoal, and brown. This is what you want if you plan on wearing your vest as transitional outerwear through the fall, winter, and spring. The other option is to go bold with a bright yellow, orange, or red. You won’t get as much use out of these, vests but they will be fun standout accessories.

Insulation: If you live in a colder climate, you should go with an insulated vest. Either a puffer or quilted vest will help lock in your body heat around your neck and torso. Known as your core, keeping this area of your body warm has the remarkable ability to make the rest of your body feel warmer because that is where all your blood circulates from.

Pockets: Pretty much all vests have a set of hand pockets. Lighter weight vests that are used for layering or warmer climates usually don’t have anything more. If you live somewhere a little colder and plan on wearing your vest as a primary outerwear option, we recommend getting one with more cargo space. One or two breast pockets, ideally with a zipper or snap closure, will always come in handy for things like sunglasses, keys, and hats.

How to Style Winter Vests

The best winter vests are strong stand-alone outerwear options that can also complete your outfit. When it isn’t quite cold enough for a winter jacket, you want a sleeveless option that will show off whatever shirt you wear underneath. The best tops to rock underneath a winter vest are sweaters, hoodies, and button-down shirts.

For sweaters, you can go high end with a cashmere crewneck or rugged with a wool cable knit sweater. If you’re going to wear a button down, it should be something a little heavier like a strong oxford cloth or denim shirt.

When going out sans jacket, we also recommend having a warm beanie and winter gloves handy incase it gets colder than expected. If your vest has a breast pocket, you can always tuck the hat or gloves into there like a pocket square.
