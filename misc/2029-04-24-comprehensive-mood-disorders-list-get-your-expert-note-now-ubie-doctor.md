Mood disorders are mental health conditions that significantly affect a person's emotional state, often disrupting daily life, relationships, and overall well-being. Common types include major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder, persistent depressive disorder (dysthymia), cyclothymic disorder, and seasonal affective disorder (SAD). Symptoms can range from prolonged sadness and hopelessness to extreme mood swings between highs (mania) and lows (depression).
