Why Discuss Risks?

Informed Choices: When doctors explain the risks, patients can choose the best birth control for their health and lifestyle.

Safety: Understanding risks helps avoid health problems. Some birth control can have side effects, and knowing them can help in making safe decisions. If you're experiencing unusual symptoms, you can use a free symptom checker to better understand what might be happening with your body.

Avoiding Unplanned Pregnancies: Knowing all options and risks helps prevent unplanned pregnancies, which can happen if birth control is not used correctly.

In conclusion, talking about birth control risks helps patients be safe and make choices that are best for them.
