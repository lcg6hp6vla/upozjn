# This Kettlebell and Bodyweight Workout Is Harder Than it Looks

There's a reason why trainer Eric Leija is at almost half a million followers on Instagram—his well-choreographed, muscle building workouts are efficient, and his videos allow his followers to try the routines for themselves at home, or on the go. The Primal Swoledier, as he's also called, recently posted a bodyweight and kettlebell workout that will give you a "serious leg and core pump" as Leija explained in his caption.

Ebenezer Samuel, C.S.C.S., Men's Health's fitness director, weighed in on how you can replicate Leija's moves at home to take on this hybrid workout yourself:

"The first move here is harder than it looks," Samuel says. "On the bear crawl pushup, think of keeping your elbows close to your torso. Meanwhile, open your knees as wide as possible."

Samuel cautions that Leija's next maneuver is also tricky: "The kickthrough move that follows will catch you off-guard as well. Take your time on it just as Eric is doing, and don't be afraid to let your front foot touch the ground."

The kettlebell portion of the workout come last, and Samuel says its a little easier to perfect. "It's as simple as it comes: Double-kettlebell swing to clean to front squat," Samuel explains. "It's a perfectly balanced flow in that you get to focus on driving from the hips with the swing, then attack your quads more aggressively with the squat."

Leija recommends that you perform each move for 5 sets, taking 30 seconds to work each movement. Rest for 1 to 2 minutes in between sets.
