# Look and Learn: Thomas Jane

Thomas Jane has always existed outside the confines of typical Hollywoodleading men. He's been an anti-hero in Punisher, sports legend MickeyMantle and a high school teacher who capitalizes on his, er, largess onHBO's Hung. His choices have always kept us guessing. So too, some of hisstyle decisions. While there's much to laud about his look, we do have a fewquestions.

1. We loved him on Hung but these sideburns are really dragging him down. His casual hairstyle is closely cropped but the ‘burns are a bit long. As with clothing, proportion is essential to looking good. In this case, Thomas has a longer, square face. He’d be better served with shorter, neater sideburns that hit mid-ear.
