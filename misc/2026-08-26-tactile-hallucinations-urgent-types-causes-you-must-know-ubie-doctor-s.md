Tactile hallucinations are sensations that feel like something is touching or moving on the skin, even though nothing is there. These can happen for different reasons:

[Must Know Ubie](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Drug-Induced Tactile Hallucinations: Some drugs can cause these feelings. This is not only from recreational drugs but also from certain medications. People might feel bugs crawling on their skin, a sensation sometimes called "formication."

Parkinson's Disease: People with Parkinson's disease might experience tactile hallucinations. This can be due to changes in the brain or as a side effect of the medications used to treat Parkinson's.

[Tactile Hallucinations Urgent Types](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/)

Delusion of Parasitosis: This is a mental health condition where people believe they are infested with bugs, leading to chronic tactile hallucinations. They might feel itching or crawling sensations on their skin.
