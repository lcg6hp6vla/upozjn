If you watched Parks and Recreation, then you know Chris Pratt has musical talent to go along with his acting chops ("5,000 Candles in the Wind," anyone?).

Pratt released his inner musician at the iHeartRadio Music Awards on Thursday night, when Garth Brooks pulled him onstage to help sing his 1990 hit song, "Friends in Low Places." The actor got really into it, belting out a section of the chorus on his own.

The Guardians of the Galaxy star had just finished introducing Brooks, who won the Artist of the Decade award.

"I bought every one of his albums and I had the lyrics memorized by the very next day," he told the crowd, according to Today.

"When I met (him), I wanted to return the favor. I wanted to give him a fraction of the joy he's given me for so many years. I told him, 'Garth, you ever need a leading man to play you in a movie I can make that happen.' True story, he looked me square in the eyes and he said 'Really? You know Bradley Cooper?'"

After the show, Pratt gushed about the experience in an Instagram post.

"Welp. I guess I died and gone to heaven," he wrote. "Thank you @garthbrooks and congratulations on the artist of the decade award. It was an honor to sing with you and the band on stage. I can’t wait for the upcoming stadium tour. God is so good. Thank you thank you thank you 😭🙏♥️👍🏼"

He also shared a backstage video with Brooks and his band. "Here I am all misty eyed backstage," he wrote. "A moment I will never forget."
