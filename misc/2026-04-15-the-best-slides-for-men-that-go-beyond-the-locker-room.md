What to Consider

Material

When buying slides, men should consider materials that suit your lifestyle. Below, we broke down the most popular types to choose from.

EVA (Ethylene Vinyl Acetate)

Lightweight and flexible, EVA provides excellent cushioning and comfort, making it a popular choice for casual and athletic slides.

PU (Polyurethane)

Known for its durability and comfort, PU offers great support and can withstand wear and tear, making it ideal for everyday use.

Leather

Genuine or synthetic leather provides a stylish and sophisticated look, with a longer lifespan than rubber or foam that can degrade faster and lose their shape. It’s suitable for casual or dressy occasions, but because of its breathable construction, it may not always be ideal for wet environments.

Rubber

Durable and water-resistant, rubber is perfect for slides intended for poolside or beach days. It provides a solid grip and is easy to clean.

Foam

Memory foam or other advanced foams can offer top-notch cushioning and comfort, molding to the shape of your foot for a customizable fit.

Fit

Slides should fit pretty snugly, but not tightly. Your feet should not hang off the edge, and the strap should hold your foot securely in place without causing discomfort. Sandals like Birkenstock’s Arizona have adjustable straps for a customizable fit.
