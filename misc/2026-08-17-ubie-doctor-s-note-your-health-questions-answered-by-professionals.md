UARS in Women 40-50: Symptoms, Home Relief & Vital Next Steps

Upper Airway Resistance Syndrome (UARS) in women ages 40–50 is common but frequently missed. Key signs include light, fragmented sleep, frequent awakenings, mild snoring, teeth grinding, morning headaches, jaw or facial pain, brain fog, mood changes, and lingering fatigue despite adequate time in bed.
