"Packing in my book isn't rocket science, but once you get a method down, it can become pretty automatic and simple.

Formulate your packing plan: I line the bottom of my suitcase with my shoes, then fill all the gaps with socks (easy to tell which and what kind of socks you need when you're staring at the shoes you're going to need them with), as well as underwear. That leaves more room for the folded clothes, which I generally like to iron before I pack, because believe it or not, clothes don't get that wrinkled in transit. It's not like they're going to move around in that jam-packed suitcase. Ideally you can fit the folded clothes on one half of the suitcase, leaving the other half for toiletries and random stuff.

[Ready For Takeoff](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/0445242840.html)

Keep these close: I always pack and take one Emergen-C vitamin packet on every flight to ward off any germs. I'm also a big fan of moist towelettes (anything with a nice fragrance is an added bonus), for everything from cleaning off a sticky spot on your seat, to hydrating dry hands. Tons of lotion and lip balm is also important on flights, since the plane air can be really drying.

Curate your carry-on: I always pack an extra set of underwear, and a clean shirt or two in my carry-on in case my checked luggage gets lost. Same goes for toiletries — make sure to at least have the bare minimum in case you are indefinitely separated from your luggage.”

If you’re traveling abroad, it pays to be paranoid. I usually take it a step further and pack at least one outfit and clothes to sleep in. It takes only one bad experience of being separated from your luggage to learn this lesson.
