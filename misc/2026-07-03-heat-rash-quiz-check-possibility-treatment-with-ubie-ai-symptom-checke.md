What is Heat Rash?

This condition occurs when areas of skin become itchy and inflamed due to excessive sweating, which blocks sweat glands.

Typical Symptoms of Heat Rash

Itchy skin

Skin rashes with tiny bumps

Heat rash

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Blisters on the skin

Scratched skin until damaged

Skin problems

Redness, bumps, or irritation on the skin

Diagnostic Questions for Heat Rash

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have itchy skin?

Do you have a skin rash with tiny bumps?

Do you have a heat rash?

Are there any skin problems on your stomach, chest, or back?

Do you have any blisters on your skin?

Treatment of Heat Rash

Keep the skin cool by wearing light, loose clothing and avoiding hot baths. Calamine lotions and steroid creams can help if symptoms are severe.
