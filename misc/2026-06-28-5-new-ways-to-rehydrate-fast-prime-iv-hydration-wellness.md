What is Dehydration?

Dehydration is a condition that happens when an individual’s body does not have even fluids to keep it functioning properly. This occurs when the body starts losing more fluids than it is taking in, leading to a variety of health issues. There are many reasons why dehydration can happen, including excessive sweating, illness such as diarrhea or fever, certain food such as salty foods or sugary snacks, and of course, not drinking enough water.

The symptoms of dehydration can range from thirst, feeling fatigued, dry mouth, feeling dizzy or lightheaded, headache, decrease in urine output, and dark urine. Young children and the elderly are more susceptible to dehydration. This is due to young and older people’s bodies having a reduced ability to regulate temperature and diminished thirst response.
