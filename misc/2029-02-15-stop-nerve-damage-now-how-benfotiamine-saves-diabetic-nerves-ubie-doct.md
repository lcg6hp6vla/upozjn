What Is Benfotiamine and How Does It Help Diabetic Nerves?

Diabetic neuropathy—nerve damage caused by chronically high blood sugar—can lead to pain, tingling, numbness and reduced quality of life. Managing blood sugar remains the cornerstone of prevention, but supplements such as benfotiamine have shown promise in supporting nerve health. Below, we explain what benfotiamine is, explore its mechanisms of action, outline the key benfotiamine benefits for diabetic nerves, and review clinical evidence you can discuss with your healthcare provider.

What Is Benfotiamine?

Benfotiamine is a lipid-soluble form of vitamin B₁ (thiamine). Unlike regular thiamine, benfotiamine:

Crosses cell membranes more easily

Achieves higher blood and tissue levels

Supports metabolic pathways with greater efficiency

Because of this enhanced absorption, benfotiamine has been studied for its potential to protect nerves and blood vessels from the harmful consequences of high glucose levels.

Clinical Evidence

In a study published in Diabetes Care, participants taking 300 mg of benfotiamine twice daily for six weeks showed significant improvement in neuropathic pain scores compared to placebo.

Another trial combining benfotiamine with other B vitamins demonstrated enhanced nerve conduction and symptom relief over eight weeks.

Patients on daily benfotiamine (150–600 mg) for 12 months maintained reduced AGE levels and stable nerve function.

Laboratory studies confirm benfotiamine's role in activating transketolase and reducing oxidative damage in cultured nerve cells.

While results are encouraging, larger and longer trials would help define optimal dosing and confirm long-term benefits.

Recommended Dosage and Usage

Typical doses range from 150 mg to 600 mg per day, often divided into two or three smaller doses.

Benfotiamine is generally taken with meals to enhance absorption and reduce any potential gastrointestinal discomfort.

Many supplement formulations combine benfotiamine with other B vitamins (B6, B12) to support overall nerve health.

Always follow the dosing instructions on your supplement label and discuss any changes with your healthcare provider.
