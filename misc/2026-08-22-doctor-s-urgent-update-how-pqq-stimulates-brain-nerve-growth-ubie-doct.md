What Is PQQ?

PQQ, or Pyrroloquinoline Quinone, is a small molecule naturally found in:

Fermented foods (e.g., natto, tempeh)

Fruits and vegetables (e.g., kiwi, spinach)

Human breast milk

It functions as an antioxidant and a redox cofactor, playing a key role in energy metabolism and cellular health.

Why Nerve Growth Factor Matters

Nerve growth factor (NGF) is a protein critical for:

Development, maintenance, and survival of neurons

Repair and regeneration of nerve tissue

Cognitive functions such as learning and memory

A decline in NGF levels is linked to age-related cognitive changes. That's why boosting NGF is a promising strategy for supporting brain health.

How PQQ Supports NGF and Nerve Growth

Mitochondrial Biogenesis

PQQ activates pathways that signal cells to produce new mitochondria.

More mitochondria means better cellular energy, which neurons need to grow and function.

Antioxidant Defense

PQQ neutralizes free radicals in and around neurons.

Reducing oxidative stress protects nerve cells from damage and supports a healthy environment for NGF activity.

Upregulation of Growth Factors

Preclinical studies show PQQ increases NGF expression in brain regions tied to memory and learning.

Animal research also indicates elevated levels of brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), another key supporter of neuronal health.

Anti-Inflammatory Effects

PQQ dampens inflammatory signaling that can inhibit NGF production.

A balanced neuroimmune environment allows for optimal nerve repair and growth.

Evidence from Research

While human clinical data remain limited, several credible studies illuminate PQQ's potential:

Cell Culture Models:Researchers observed increased NGF gene expression in cultured neuronal cells treated with PQQ.

Rodent Studies:In rats, dietary PQQ led to higher brain NGF levels and improvements in maze-based memory tasks.

Mitochondrial Health Trials:Small human trials report enhanced energy metabolism and cognitive test performance with PQQ supplementation, suggesting indirect support for nerve growth.

Key takeaway: Early data are promising, but larger, long-term human studies are needed to confirm the optimal doses and full range of benefits.

Potential Benefits of PQQ for Brain Health

Using PQQ for nerve growth factor support may help with:

Enhanced learning and memory

Improved mental clarity and focus

Protection against age-related cognitive decline

Practical Tips for Maximizing NGF Support

Pair PQQ with foods rich in omega-3s (e.g., salmon, flaxseed) to enhance neuronal membrane health.

Include polyphenol-rich fruits and vegetables to further reduce oxidative stress.

Stay physically active; exercise itself raises NGF and BDNF levels.

Prioritize sleep hygiene—deep sleep is when your brain repairs and regenerates neurons.
