Numbness in the upper right abdomen is often caused by nerve irritation in the skin or abdominal muscles. In some cases, it may signal a deeper issue such as inflammation, nerve compression, or a blood vessel condition like polyarteritis nodosa. Identifying the underlying cause is important, since treatment depends on whether the source is nerve-related, muscular, or systemic.

Explanation

There are a few possible reasons for feeling numbness in the upper right belly area. One possibility is that nerves in the skin or the muscles are being pressed or stretched, which might cause a tingling or numb feeling. Sometimes, problems like a hernia or inflammation in nearby tissues can affect these nerves.
