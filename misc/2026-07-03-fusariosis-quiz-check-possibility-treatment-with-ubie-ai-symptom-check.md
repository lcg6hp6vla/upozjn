What is Fusariosis?

Fusariosis refers to a range of infections in humans caused by a fungus from the Fusarium species. These infections can be either locally invasive or widespread in the body. People with weakened immune systems are at a higher risk.

Typical Symptoms of Fusariosis

History of diabetes requiring medications

Change in nail color

Fever

New bumps appearing while some bumps are healing already

Finger pain

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Bumpy rash that appeared, then rapidly spread to the rest of the body

Skin problem of the legs, in the area between thigh and ankle

Diagnostic Questions for Fusariosis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you ever been treated for diabetes?

Did your nail color change?

Do you have a fever?

Are some of your rashes healing while others are appearing?

Are you experiencing pain in your finger?

Treatment of Fusariosis

Fusarium disease is treated with antifungal medication.
