What type of doctor should I see for persistent elbow pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Elbow pain that limits movement or persists beyond a few days should be evaluated by a sports medicine physician or an orthopedic surgeon specializing in elbow injuries. These specialists can perform a physical exam, order imaging if needed, accurately diagnose the underlying cause—such as tendonitis, ligament strain, or nerve involvement—and recommend a personalized treatment plan.
