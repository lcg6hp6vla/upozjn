# French Montana Responds to Accusations That He Spray-Painted His Abs

[to Accusations That He](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

French Montana is proud of the work he’s put in over the past few months to achieve—and maintain—his newly ripped physique, and he’s not about to let a few haters bring him down.

Just over a year after the rapper unexpectedly spent some time in the ICU recovering from a heart condition, he celebrated his road to a healthier lifestyle by debuting his new and improved physique on the cover of the newest digital issue of XXL Magazine.

While no one can deny the rapper looks great in the photo (which he revealed late last month), some naysayers are refusing to believe the abs he has on display are actually his. In fact, they’re going so far as to say Montana can thank spray paint for his chiseled core.

Let’s just say Montana is not exactly pleased with those accusations, and he’s decided to let his haters know exactly what he thinks.

As Montana revealed in a behind-the-scenes video of his cover shoot with the publication, his six-pack is most definitely real, because he knows better than to lie to his social media following about taking better care of his health.

“They said I spray painted my six pack on 🤦‍♂️,” he captioned the video on Instagram. “I got catfished too many times to catfish my people lol I just got to it! Nothing beats hard work and always remember there’s some people out there that don’t want you to be great.”

Since going “French 2.0,” as he put it, Montana says he's cut out alcohol, took some time off from social media, and stuck to a more consistent gym regimen.

“When I went to the hospital, they were like ‘if you drink, you’ll die.’ That’s why I took a break,” he explained to the publication about his former lifestyle. “I think that was like the hardest thing I ever did in my life, to snatch myself back. So, for me to stop everything and just step back, that was like one of my biggest accomplishments."
