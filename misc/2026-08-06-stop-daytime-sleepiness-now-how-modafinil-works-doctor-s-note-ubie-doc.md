How Modafinil Works

Brain Chemicals: Modafinil changes the way some brain chemicals work. These chemicals, like dopamine and norepinephrine, help keep us awake and alert.

Wakefulness: By affecting these chemicals, modafinil helps people with narcolepsy feel more awake during the day.
