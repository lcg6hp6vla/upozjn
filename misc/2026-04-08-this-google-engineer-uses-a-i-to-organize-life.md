# This Google Engineer Uses A.I. to Organize Life

YOU'VE PROBABLY HEARD of Google’s AI chatbot, Bard. Well, Bard has a “teacher”: Jack Krawczyk, director of product management. In the chatbot arms race against other tech titans, Krawczyk is under intense pressure. Yet he has found a way to harness his and Bard’s skills to make the stress a bit more manageable.

[Organize Life](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/4107879521.html)

6:30 A.M.: Skim and Bob

After a quick skim for overnight emails, I use morning as my sacred time to put my phone away, collect my thoughts, and connect with family—sometimes over Bob Marley. When the kids, ages five and two, get up, we pick a record to listen to, and that’s a current favorite.

8:00 A.M.: Get Clarity

Bard helps me set the tone for the day. I tend to think in unstructured ways, so often I’ll open the mic and record myself thinking out loud for two to three minutes. Then I’ll ask Bard to synthesize my thoughts in three to four bullet points and use that to set my priorities.

11:00 A.M.: Say Yes...Ish

I ruthlessly prioritize by constantly thinking about trade-offs. For example, I’ll make it clear that “I can support x, but I need to trade off y to do it.” This forces me to frame my work in terms of priorities and to share those priorities with others.

1:00 P.M.: Find Harmony

[Google Engineer](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/)

[This Google Engineer Uses](https://allpepay.com/)

Everyone says you need work/life balance, but I prefer the notion of harmony: I get energy from my work. I feel more energized toward the end of the day than at the beginning, especially on a day when I feel like we made a breakthrough, like the first time I got to demo Bard transforming an image to text.

3:00 P.M.: Run it Out

I hate indoor exercise, so I try to move my feet outdoors as much as I can. On home days, I’ll take any one-hour break I find and do a five-mile run. If I throw on a podcast—a rotation of Armchair Expert, Smartless, and the Lex Fridman Podcast—it serves double time as my meditation.

7:00 P.M.: Date Night

[Google Engineer](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/)

My wife and I have a standing Thursday date. We make an effort to bring unique conversation topics to the table; it’s so easy to default to talking about the kids and work. Instead, we use this time to discuss other topics, like books, music, pop culture, or fashion trends.

8:00 P.M.: Tell a Story

Bedtime is my ideal time with my sons. Sometimes I’ll use Bard to co-create bedtime stories it reads aloud to them. I’ve learned there are never-ending ways to tell stories about construction vehicles.

10:00 P.M.: Plug Away

My best nights of sleep are when I charge my phone away from the nightstand. I often fail at this, but keep trying.
