# ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 5 Has Already Been Filmed

The fourth season of Love Is Blind, Netflix's hit social experiment turned dating reality show, recently came to a close, having delivered another satisfying round of awkward pod chat, partner-swapping, relationship drama, and for a select few couples, a happily-ever-after ending.

While Chelsea and Kwame were able to overcome the initial tension in their marriage (due to his mother's reluctance to accept her), and Tiffany and Brett continue to be the golden couple of the season, other cast members have taken their time away from the show as an opportunity to reflect on their behavior.

In the recent After the Altar reunion special, we saw how this year's cast are all getting on. "Mean girl" Irina has apologized for the way she acted towards her fellow contestants and towards Micah in particular, and is now trying to reclaim both the nickname "Bean" and her title as "villain." And Jackie has made certain to clear up the facts about the end of her relationship with Marshall—although her subsequent courtship with Josh and fallout with Jackie have both been just as fraught.

All of that drama may have sated some viewers, but for most, it just left them wanting more, and now the fanbase is eagerly awaiting the next season.
